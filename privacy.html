Privacy policy

Short version: your data stays on your PC, except what Microsoft and Mojang need to sign you in.

What the launcher stores

On your computer only, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\EaglerJavaLauncher:

  • your Minecraft profile name and UUID, and your Xbox user ID;
  • a Microsoft refresh token and a Minecraft access token, encrypted for your Windows user with DPAPI;
  • your settings (game version, memory, server list) and the game files.

Your password is never seen or stored: you type it on Microsoft's own page.

Where data is sent

  • Microsoft (login.microsoftonline.com) and Xbox Live: to sign you in.
  • Minecraft services (api.minecraftservices.com): to get your profile and game token.
  • Mojang, Fabric and Modrinth download servers: to download the game, its libraries and optional mods. No personal data is sent.
  • Minecraft servers you choose to join: the game itself connects to them.

The launcher has no analytics, no telemetry, no ads and no server of its own. Nothing is sold or shared.

Deleting your data

Click "Sign out" in the launcher to delete the stored tokens, or delete the %LOCALAPPDATA%\EaglerJavaLauncher folder to remove everything. You can also revoke the app at any time from your Microsoft account's app permissions page.
